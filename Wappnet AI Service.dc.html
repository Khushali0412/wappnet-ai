AI that survives contact with production

We take one high-value use case, prove it against your own data, then engineer it into a service your team can run, monitor and explain.

Scope a use case Related work

Most AI programmes stall between demo and deployment

The model usually is not the hard part. The hard part is the data it depends on, the evaluation set that tells you whether it is getting better, and the operational answer to what happens when it is wrong at 3am.

We start where the value is measurable and the data already exists. One use case, a scoring set agreed with the people who will be judged on the output, and a deployment path signed off by whoever owns the platform. After that we build.

What we build

LLM application engineering

How we work

Four stages, and you can stop after any of them

01 — FRAME

Pick the one use case

Value, data readiness, and who signs off on the output. Two weeks.

02 — PROVE

Test against real data

A scored prototype on your data, with a go or no-go number. Four to six weeks.

03 — ENGINEER

Make it a service

Pipelines, interfaces, auth, monitoring and cost controls in your cloud.

04 — OPERATE

Hand it over or run it

Your team takes it, or we operate it on an agreed support window.
