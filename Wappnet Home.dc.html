A first production release usually takes 10 to 16 weeks: two weeks to frame the use case, four to six to prove it on your data, and the remainder to engineer and deploy it. Narrow automations ship sooner; regulated environments take longer because of review cycles, not build time.
No. We work with the data you already have and fix only what the chosen use case depends on. A full data cleanup before a first project typically delays value by a year without changing the outcome.
The two-week AI opportunity assessment is a fixed fee, and every stage after it is quoted separately. You can stop at the end of any stage without a contractual argument.
We rank candidates on business value, data readiness and effort, then start with the highest-value one your existing data already supports. Anything that needs new data collection gets sequenced later.
You do, from the first commit. Work happens in your repositories and your cloud accounts, under your licence terms, so nothing is locked behind our tooling.
Senior pods of three to six engineers who have shipped the same class of system before. The people on your first call stay on the account through delivery.
Yes. We work inside your repo, ticket tracker and coding standards, and treat runbooks and architecture notes as deliverables rather than favours.
